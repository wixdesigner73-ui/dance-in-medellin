Dance Experience & Skill Levels

Do I need prior salsa or bachata experience to join a dance immersion in Medellín?

No. Complete beginners and experienced dancers are welcome. Your one-on-one lessons are built around your level. Group classes bring everyone together, while hosted socials give you a chance to practice at your own pace—watching and listening count, too.

What style will I learn?

If you have a style you'd like to focus on—such as salsa on1, on2, Cuban, or Cali-style, or a particular style of bachata—tell me when you register. I'll confirm the teaching team's specialties and what's possible for that cohort. If you're unsure, that's fine too.

What if I’m nervous about dancing socially?

It can be intimidating to use new steps at a social. That's why local practice partners or instructors join our two hosted nights to dance with guests and help bridge the gap between class and the real floor. They're shared among the group, not each person's exclusive partner for the evening. You can also watch, rest, or dance independently.

How do the private lessons work?

You'll have ten hours of one-on-one instruction, usually two hours a day on Monday through Friday. Your teacher adapts the sessions to your level, goals, and what they notice in your dancing.

One person might want to work on timing and connection, while someone else may focus on body movement, musicality, partnerwork, confidence, or simply feeling more comfortable on the dance floor.

Is social dancing included?

Yes. We organize two hosted nights out, including entry fees and local practice-partner support. You can choose whether to dance every song, enjoy the music, or call it a night earlier.

Is this a salsa or bachata retreat?

Not exactly! Think of it as a small-group dance vacation that combines one-on-one training, local social dancing, and cultural experiences. There's room to improve your dancing, explore Medellín, and enjoy your own free time without an all-day schedule.

Solo Travelers, Couples & the Group

Do I need to come with a partner?

No partner is required. Solo travelers are absolutely welcome.

Can couples join?

Yes. Couples are welcome, and you do not need to dance at the same level.

How big are the groups?

Groups are intentionally small (5-8 people) so people can actually get to know one another and the week can stay flexible.

Who am I spending the week with?

Most participants arrive knowing little or nothing about the others. With a group of 5–8 people who share an interest in dancing and exploring Medellín, you'll have company when you want it and room to do your own thing.

Will I have free time?

Yes. There is structure, but there is also room to nap after a late social, explore on your own, meet a friend, or simply have an unscheduled afternoon.

Do I have to do every activity?

No. Some moments are central to the shared experience, but you’ll also have independence and free time.

Language & Support

Do I need to know Spanish?

No. I'll help bridge language gaps during the trip, and I'll confirm how much English the instructors for your specific cohort speak. Knowing a few Spanish phrases is a bonus, not a requirement.

Will Nina be there during the immersion?

Yes. I'll host the trip in person, coordinate scheduled activities, help you get oriented, and be available for questions or practical issues that come up during the week. I'm not a 24-hour concierge, but you won't be handed an itinerary and left on your own.

Medellín & Safety

Is Medellín safe?

Medellín is a major city with real safety considerations. I won't promise a risk-free trip.

Before arrival, I'll share practical guidance on getting around, common precautions, and what to expect. Airport transfers and transportation for included activities are arranged; we go to our two hosted socials together. I'll be there during the trip to help with questions, but ordinary travel risks still apply.

Will I be okay traveling to Medellín alone?

Absolutely. Solo travelers are welcome, and you won't be the only person arriving without a dance partner.

You'll have an arranged airport pickup on the scheduled arrival date, your own suite, a small group to meet, and me on the ground to help you get oriented.

Accommodation, Transportation & Travel

Where will we stay?

You'll stay at Origen Local Suites in Medellín's Laureles neighborhood, within walking distance of the dance school, restaurants, cafés, and places to dance.

Each suite includes a kitchen, air conditioning, a private bathroom, and high-speed Wi-Fi.

What's the difference between shared and private accommodation?

There are two options, with the same training, activities, and hosting included:

Shared Suite ($2,199 per person): For two people booking together. Choose two Twin XL beds or one king bed.
Private Suite ($2,399 per person): Enjoy the entire suite to yourself. This is the option for solo guests.

Both options include the same dance training, cultural activities, and support throughout the week.

Can I book a shared suite if I'm traveling alone?

Shared suite pricing is for two guests who book together. I don't match solo guests with roommates; if you're coming on your own, please choose a private suite.

You'll still have plenty of opportunities to connect with the group during lessons, outings, and social dancing.

Can I share a suite with my friend or partner?

Absolutely! Friends, couples, and other travel companions are welcome to book together.

You can choose between two Twin XL beds or one king bed, depending on what works best for you.

Do I have to share accommodation with other participants?

Not unless you choose to! The private suite option gives you your own space for the entire week.

Even though we're traveling as a small group, everyone has a comfortable place to retreat to at the end of the day.

Where does the trip start and end?

Included transfers run to and from José María Córdova International Airport (MDE) for the trip's scheduled arrival and departure dates.

Can I arrive early or stay longer?

Absolutely. You’re welcome to extend your time in Medellín before or after the official trip dates.

Additional accommodation, transportation, and activities outside the immersion dates are your responsibility.

What travel documents are needed?

Entry rules vary by nationality, length of stay, and circumstances, and they can change. Please check current requirements with the Colombian immigration authorities and your airline before booking travel; don't assume a passport is the only document you'll need.

If you're traveling on a non-U.S. passport, check the official guidance for your nationality or contact the relevant Colombian embassy or consulate.

Do I need travel insurance?

Travel insurance isn't included, and I strongly recommend considering coverage for medical needs, cancellations, and other unexpected expenses. Coverage varies, so read the policy terms carefully.

Is the trip suitable if I have mobility or accessibility needs?

Please contact me before paying a deposit to discuss your needs. Parts of the week may include walking, stairs, late-night activities, and dance venues with different accessibility conditions. I'll check what's feasible with the hotel and local partners rather than promise accommodations I haven't verified.

What’s Included

What’s included in the price?

Seven nights' accommodation in your selected suite, ten private lesson hours, two group classes, two hosted social-dance nights (including admissions and practice-partner support), locally hosted cultural experiences, a welcome gathering, airport transfers, transportation to included activities, and my pre-trip and on-the-ground hosting.

What’s NOT included in the price?

Flights, travel insurance, hotel incidentals, laundry service, and any food, drinks, or activities not included in the itinerary.

Are meals included?

A hosted food experience is included. Other meals are generally on your own unless the confirmed itinerary explicitly lists them. You'll have plenty of nearby choices.

Pricing, Payments & Cancellations

Is there a deposit?

There's no payment to submit your interest form. Once dates, availability, and terms are confirmed and you decide to book, a $500 deposit is required to reserve your spot.

When will my final balance be due?

The $500 booking deposit confirms your spot, and the balance is due 30 days before departure. I'll share the payment schedule and full refund terms before you pay.

What is the cancellation policy?

Under the proposed policy, cancellations more than 30 days before departure receive a refund of payments made minus the $500 nonrefundable deposit. Please see the full Cancellation & Refund Policy for details. If your plans change, reach out! I'll do my best to help you find a solution.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. You may pay in installments as long as your balance is paid in full 30 days before departure. If you'd like to pay by bank transfer, Zelle, or Venmo, ask me for the available options.
