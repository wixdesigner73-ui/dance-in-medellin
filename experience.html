10 hours · one-on-one

Private lessons that are actually about you

You'll receive ten hours of one-on-one private instruction: two hours a day, Monday through Friday. If ten hours doesn’t seem enough, add-ons are available.

Your instructor adapts to your level and goals. You might focus on timing, leading or following, body movement, musicality, partnerwork, or getting comfortable with the basics. You don't need a dance partner, and you won't follow a preset curriculum.

By the end of the week, the goal is not simply to have taken ten hours of class. It’s to notice that something feels better the next time your favorite song comes on.

2 hosted social nights

Then you take it onto the dance floor

Two evenings, we'll explore Medellín's salsa or bachata scene together, joined by local practice partners and instructors to help you build confidence.

If dancing with strangers feels intimidating, you won't be alone. Our practice partners will help you apply what you've learned and feel comfortable using them on the dance floor.

Dance with our group, meet new people, watch the scene, or simply enjoy the music. There's no pressure to perform.

Food · culture · people

Medellín beyond the studio

Some favorite moments happen away from the dance floor.

Our first trip included a Colombian food experience and a memorable introduction to local fruit. Those are the kinds of activities I love: hands-on, personal, and grounded in everyday life here.

Future weeks will include a locally hosted food experience and another cultural activity chosen with our Colombian partners. The specific activities may change, and I'll share confirmed plans before booking.

Who tends to love this trip

You’ll probably feel at home here if you:

  • Want to improve your dancing without spending your whole vacation in class
  • Are curious about Medellín beyond the obvious tourist stops
  • Like small groups and getting to know the people you travel with
  • Are traveling solo and like the idea of arriving with built-in community
  • Want support without having every minute scheduled

This probably isn't the right fit if you're looking for a party tour, a nonstop dance bootcamp, a big sightseeing group, or a luxury retreat.
