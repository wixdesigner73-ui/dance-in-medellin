A week in rhythm

What the week feels like

Your morning might start with a private lesson focused entirely on your dancing. By night, you’re trying those ideas on a crowded social floor, hearing the same music differently because you spent the afternoon breaking it down with your instructor.

Between lessons and socials, there’s time to explore Medellín: share a meal, wander a neighborhood, try something local, or sit with the group and trade stories about what happened on the dance floor the night before.

The week has a plan, but your free time is still yours. You're welcome to rest, explore, or stay out for another dance.
