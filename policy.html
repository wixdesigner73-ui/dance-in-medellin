Cancellation & Refund Policy

I understand that plans can change! Here's what you can expect if you need to cancel your trip.

If you need to cancel

More than 30 days before the trip start date

You'll receive a refund of all payments made, minus your $500 non-refundable deposit.

30 days or fewer before the trip start date

Payments are non-refundable. However, I understand that unexpected things happen, and I'll do my best to help you explore the following options:

  • Transfer your spot: You may transfer your booking to another eligible person with my approval, provided the necessary arrangements can be transferred. One transfer is permitted per booking.
  • Future-trip credit: Depending on which costs can be recovered, I may be able to offer credit toward a future Dance in Medellín trip. Credits exclude the original $500 deposit and any non-recoverable expenses.

Using future-trip credits

  • Credits are valid for 12 months from the date they're issued and must be applied to a new booking within that period.
  • If the new trip costs more, you'll pay the difference.
  • Credits cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Exceptions may be made at my discretion.

If I need to cancel the trip

In the extremely unlikely event that I cancel the entire trip, you'll receive a full refund of everything you've paid me, including your deposit.

Alternatively, you may choose to apply your payments toward a future trip.

If I'm unexpectedly unable to host personally, I'll arrange for a qualified, trusted local host to step in whenever reasonably possible, with the goal of preserving the planned experience. I'll communicate any changes with you.

A few important details

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing.
  • The cancellation deadline is based on the official trip start date, regardless of when you personally travel to Colombia.
  • Flights, travel insurance, and other independently booked expenses aren't included in refunds.
  • I strongly recommend considering travel insurance to protect yourself against unexpected changes.

Have questions or need to change your plans? Please reach out! I'll do my best to help you find a solution.
