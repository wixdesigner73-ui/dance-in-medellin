Guest Stories From Our Medellín Dance Immersion

What was it actually like?

A week of dancing in Medellín looks a little different for everyone. Here’s what stood out to some of our first participants.

Dimple

“It felt like we were on a trip with wonderful friends.”

Dimple traveled to Colombia specifically for the experience. He arrived with some concerns about safety, but said the guidance and support throughout the week helped put those worries at ease.

His favorite part was the feeling of community — along with private lessons, exploring Medellín, and getting to experience Colombian culture beyond the dance floor.

“The Colombia trip was wonderful and far exceeded my expectations.”— Dimple, August 2026 participant
Aaron

“The special experiences you can’t find by yourself.”

Aaron was initially nervous about spending a week with people he mostly didn’t know.

Instead, some of his favorite memories came from exactly that sense of connection — social dancing with local instructors, sharing meals, and experiencing Medellín through people who call it home.

“Do it!! You will have an amazing time — you will learn a ton, fully immerse, and experience great joy.”— Aaron, August 2026 participant
Carena

Taking what you learn onto the dance floor

For Carena, one of the most valuable parts of the week was being able to immediately apply what she was learning.

Private lessons, group classes, and guided social dancing gave her different ways to practice — including dancing with instructors and locals in real social settings.

She also loved that many of the cultural experiences were connected to friends, family, and local people. “It really demonstrates the warm culture of Colombia.”

Her advice for future participants?

“Be ready to book your next flight back to Medellín.”— Carena, August 2026 participant
